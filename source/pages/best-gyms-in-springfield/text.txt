LOOKING FOR THE BEST GYMS IN SPRINGFIELD, MO?

Whether you’re hunting down your next PR, chasing your first unassisted pull-up, or just want to sweat with the best community around, this guide has got you covered. Many of these spots are right around central Springfield, stretching from Downtown to the Battlefield corridor.

Offering everything from workout classes and recovery tools to elite coaching and a strong community, these are the real-deal gyms in Springfield, MO. From 24-hour access to fitness centers with pools, childcare, personal training, and more, it’s all here.

Before we get started on our list—can we talk about a gym where you won’t find rows of treadmills or the same old machines? 

Instead, think obstacle courses, warped walls, rock holds, and Ninja Warrior-style challenges. If you're ready to swing, climb,

and train like never before, check out our Adult Fitness Classes and see what makes Springfield Warrior Sports 

one of the most unique gyms around. We’re located just south of Sunshine and a quick drive from Republic and southwest Springfield.

RAGE FITNESS
2321 S Campbell Ave, Springfield, MO

If you want gyms in Springfield that don’t mess around—this is it. Rage Fitness is raw, real, and ridiculously well-equipped. Four locations, 24/7 access, and a no-excuses culture that hits different. The Rage Army doesn’t skip days, make excuses, or settle for mediocrity.

This is where grit meets grind. You’ll find private locker rooms, cold plunges, elite gear, and a staff that trains like they mean it, because at Rage, it’s NO. DAYS. OFF.

Highlights:


24/7 access — because results don’t clock out

Cold plunges, saunas, compression sleeves, tanning
Locally owned, hardcore ethos
Group classes and personal training

3119 S Scenic Ave Ste B, Springfield, MO

From elite coaching to personal training, group classes, and online programs, this place shows up for its people. Clean, welcoming, and serious about progress, it’s where 70-year-olds deadlift next to teens hitting their first squat cycle. You’ll be pushed when you need it, and lifted up if you fall.

Highlights:

Expert-led barbell and strength classes

Open gym for lifters who know their way around
Welcoming community for all ages
