WANT TO BOOK A PRIVATE LASER TAG PARTY?

Whether you are planning a birthday party, a fun youth group outing, or a team-building event, the Warrior Ninja Sports gym is the perfect place to host a full-blown laser tag fun-fest.


The best part?


Our staff will do all the setup and be on standby to offer help and assistance if needed. Our goal is to make every laser tag party the most fun, stress-free, and enjoyable experience ever.


Call us today and book our entire obstacle course gym for your very own private laser tag party. We do the rest!

HERE ARE THE DETAILS:
Cost: For a group of 10, the cost is $240. Each additional participant costs $10.
We are available for booking on weekends and most weekdays

We provide a cooler full of Gatorade for all parties. We even customize party invitations for your event, free of charge!

Plus, if you have a birthday kiddo in the group, they will receive a Springfield Warrior Sports t-shirt and a free Junior Warrior class voucher!

We want kids to have a blast, making memories that last a lifetime.

READY TO BOOK?

You can begin the booking process on our website by clicking here or calling us today at (417) 894-7319 to request a time to reserve the gym for your private party.
