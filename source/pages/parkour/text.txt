PARKOUR BENEFITS
IMPROVE PHYSICAL FITNESS:


Parkour can help to improve overall physical fitness and develop a greater strength, speed, stamina and balance. The movements involve a variety of exercises that work out several muscles at once, allowing for a complete body workout.


BUILDS SELF-CONFIDENCE:


By learning how to overcome obstacles and accomplish difficult tasks such as running, jumping and climbing, Parkour can help build self-confidence as students become more competent in their abilities. As they learn to move freely with the environment around them, they become more confident in their own abilities to adapt and succeed in new situations.


MENTAL STIMULATION:


Parkour offers an enjoyable and mentally stimulating activity that allows students to practice coordination skills while having fun at the same time. It also encourages problem solving skills as students have to find creative ways of navigating through different obstacle courses and scenarios.


STRESS RELIEF:


Parkour is a great way to reduce stress levels by letting off some steam while using physical activity as an outlet for negative emotions. The mental clarity that comes from focusing on the task at hand helps clear the mind of any distractions or worries that may be plaguing it, allowing for renewed focus and energy afterwards.


COMMUNITY:


Participants in Parkour can gain access to a strong supportive community of like-minded individuals who are all striving for personal growth through physical activity with each other's support and encouragement.
