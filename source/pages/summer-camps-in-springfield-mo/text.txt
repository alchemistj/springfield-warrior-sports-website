AT OUR INCREDIBLE SUMMER CAMPS

Looking for a worthwhile activity for your kid this summer?

Look no further! Our incredible Summer Camps in Springfield, MO are packed with fun and educational activities.

Kids will get the chance to work with our Ninja Warrior trainers, and participate in physical activities designed to enhance their balance, core, and overall strength. We provide healthy snacks each day as we educate children on making good nutritional choices that lead to improved health and wellness.

Plus, there’s a competition at every camp that awards the top place-getters a spot in the final championship at the end of summer – how cool is that?!

Come join us at our camps, which are perfect for children approximately aged 6 to 15.
