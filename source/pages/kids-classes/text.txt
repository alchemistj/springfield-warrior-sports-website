CSNA COMPETITIVE NINJA TEAM
(7-18 YEARS OLD)

The Central States Ninja Association is a competitive youth team for Junior Warriors who are ready to take it to the next level and put their skills to the test! Junior Warriors are eligible to apply for the CSNA team upon completing Level 4. The team works with Coach Adam Arnold who holds 90-minute practices 2 days a week. The season runs September - May with competitions scheduled throughout the region hosted by other participating CSNA gyms |MO, KS, NE, AR|.

Cost: $145 for two practices a week. Includes an open gym membership.

PARKOUR
(7+ YEARS OLD)

⚠️ Parkour Classes are currently unavailable.⚠️

Open Gym is still available for Parkour training.

Learn the basics of Parkour |free running| in this group class. Students will gain the ability to move freely with and without obstacles using the abilities of their bodies. This will be accomplished primarily through running, jumping, climbing, and quadrupedal movements. Classes will focus on physical conditioning and Parkour skills and will ultimately blend the two together. Experience the non-competitive mental and physical challenge while building strength, speed, stamina, and balance in a controlled environment.
